## 🎯 Your Daily Tasks

### Morning Check
1. **Open Admin Panel** → http://localhost/icecream/admin_login.php
2. **Go to Dashboard** → See pending orders count
3. **Check Orders** → Click "Orders" in sidebar
4. **Filter Pending** → Click "Pending Confirmation" tab

### Processing Orders
For each PENDING order:
1. Review order details (item, quantity, customer)
2. Click status dropdown
3. Select **"✓ Confirmed"**
4. Click **Save** button
5. Move to next order

### Shipping
When ready to ship:
1. Find CONFIRMED orders
2. Change to **"📦 Shipped"**
3. Save
4. Send tracking info to customer (if available)

### Delivery
When customer receives order:
1. Find SHIPPED orders
2. Change to **"✓✓ Delivered"**
3. Save
4. Order is complete!

## 🔴 Order Status Flow

```
⏳ PENDING
  ↓ (You approve it)
✓ CONFIRMED
  ↓ (Ready to ship)
📦 SHIPPED
  ↓ (Delivered to customer)
✓✓ DELIVERED ✅

OR

✕ CANCELLED (If needed)
```

---

## 📋 Order Information You See

- **Order ID**: Unique reference number (#1, #2, etc.)
- **Item Name**: What the customer ordered
- **Customer Name**: Who placed the order
- **Contact**: Phone number to reach them
- **Price**: Cost of one item in ₹
- **Qty**: How many they ordered
- **Order Date**: When they ordered (day & time)
- **Current Status**: What stage it's in
- **Update**: Dropdown to change status

## ⚡ Quick Tips

✅ **Best Practice**
- Confirm orders by end of day
- Ship within 24 hours of confirmation
- Mark delivered same day customer receives

⚠️ **Watch Out For**
- Duplicate orders (might be accidental add)
- Pending orders staying too long (follow up with customer)
- Wrong customer info (ask customer to update profile)
